# Code of conduct

getClosr is a tool for talking about desire and limits with care. We expect the same care in this project.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, whatever their gender, sexuality, relationship style, body, age, background or level of experience.
- Discuss kinks and practices factually and without judgement. Someone else's yes, maybe or no is not up for debate.
- Give and accept critique of the work, not of the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's codes, answers, notes or personal details, and do not ask for them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or sexual remarks aimed at a participant
- Sexual content that is unsolicited or goes beyond what a technical discussion needs
- Pressure on anyone to disclose their own preferences or limits
- Publishing someone's private information or getClosr codes without their consent
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people from the project. To report a problem, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or open an issue asking to be contacted, without details. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository. It is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
